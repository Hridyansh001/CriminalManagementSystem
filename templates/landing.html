PORTAL CAPABILITIES

Comprehensive Digital Police Infrastructure

Built to support every stage of criminal justice administration with transparent accountability.

📋

e-FIR Filing & Auto-Generation

Citizens submit complaints with automated FIR number assignment (`FIR-YYYY-XXX`), structured crime classifications, location mapping, and instant status logging.

🔍

Officer Investigation Console

Police officers update case diaries, assign suspect statuses, track investigation start & chargesheet dates, and manage assigned precinct cases.

🗂️

Criminal Intelligence Registry

Central intelligence database of convicted, wanted, and accused persons with threat classifications, known aliases, National IDs, and multi-case links.

🔒

Evidence Vault & Forensics

Cryptographically tracked chain of custody for all physical, documentary, and forensic evidence items linked to officers, lockers, and FSL labs.

🏛️

Judicial Courts & Trials

Seamless integration with court proceedings, hearing schedules, presiding judge records, and final judicial verdict / sentencing documentation.

📜

Citizen Rights & Legal Aid

Public legal charter outlining mandatory Section 154 CrPC registration rights, Zero FIR procedures, women protections, and bail guidelines.
